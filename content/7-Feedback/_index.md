## Overall Evaluation

### Working and Learning Environment

FCAJ provided a friendly learning environment where I could explore AWS and learn from other participants. The workshops and community events helped me understand how cloud services are used in real projects.

### Mentor and Program Coordinator Support

The mentors and organizers shared useful knowledge and learning resources. Their guidance helped me understand what I should focus on when studying AWS. I hope future participants will have more opportunities to ask questions and receive feedback on their projects.

### Relevance to My Major

As an Information Technology student, I found the program relevant to my studies. Working on Tindy helped me apply what I learned about databases, web development, and cloud computing.

### Learning and Skill Development

I learned how to use AWS services and gained experience working on a cloud project. I also improved my research, documentation, and presentation skills through weekly learning activities and Community Day.

### Knowledge Sharing and Teamwork

Community events gave me opportunities to learn from others and share my knowledge. I also realized that communication and teamwork are important skills that I still need to improve.

### Resources and Hands-on Access

FCAJ provided many useful learning resources, including AWS workshops and online materials. However, some practical exercises were difficult for beginners, so more step-by-step instructions would be helpful.

## What I Appreciated Most

My favorite experience was presenting **"New to AWS? Where Should You Start?"** at FCAJ Community Day. It was a valuable opportunity to practice public speaking and share what I had learned. I would recommend FCAJ to students interested in AWS and cloud computing.

## Suggestions for Future Cohorts

1. **Provide clearer learning instructions.** A simple weekly learning plan would help beginners follow the program.
2. **Offer more hands-on workshops.** Practical exercises would help students understand AWS services better.
3. **Increase mentor feedback.** More project reviews would help participants identify mistakes and improve their work.
4. **Encourage more group activities.** Small group discussions and presentations would help students improve their communication and teamwork skills.
