## 1. Overall Evaluation

### Working and Learning Environment

FCAJ provided a friendly and comfortable learning environment that encouraged independent learning. I had opportunities to explore AWS, participate in practical activities, and connect with people who share an interest in technology.

The workshops and community events helped me learn new things and better understand how technology is used in real-world projects.

### Mentor and Program Coordinator Support

The mentors and organizers shared useful knowledge, learning resources, and practical experience throughout the program. Their support helped me understand what to focus on when learning AWS and working on my project.

I hope future participants will have more opportunities to discuss their projects with mentors, ask questions, and receive feedback.

### Relevance to My Major

As an Information Technology student, I found the program relevant to my studies.

Working on Tindy allowed me to apply what I had learned about databases, web development, and cloud computing. It helped me better understand the connection between classroom knowledge and building a real application.

### Learning and Skill Development

During FCAJ, I learned about AWS services and gained experience working on a cloud project.

Besides technical knowledge, I improved my self-learning, research, documentation, and presentation skills. Participating in Community Day also gave me an opportunity to share what I had learned and become more confident when speaking in front of others.

### Knowledge Sharing and Teamwork

Community activities gave me opportunities to meet new people, exchange ideas, and learn from both experienced speakers and other participants.

While working on Tindy, I learned more about the importance of communication, coordination, and supporting team members. These are also skills that I hope to continue improving.

### Resources and Hands-on Access

FCAJ provided many useful learning resources, including AWS workshops and online materials. These resources helped me study independently and practice outside the learning sessions.

However, some hands-on exercises were challenging for beginners. I think more step-by-step instructions and simple examples would make the learning process easier.

## 2. What I Appreciated Most

What I appreciated most about FCAJ was the opportunity to learn and apply my knowledge through the Tindy project instead of only studying theory.

The workshops and Community Day were also memorable experiences. I had opportunities to listen to practical insights, meet people with similar interests, and learn more about the technology industry.

The program helped me become more confident in learning new things and motivated me to continue developing my skills.

I would recommend FCAJ to students interested in AWS, cloud computing, and gaining practical experience.

## 3. Suggestions for Future Cohorts

- **Provide clearer learning instructions:** A simple weekly learning plan would help beginners follow the program more easily.
- **Offer more hands-on workshops:** Additional practical sessions on AWS topics would help students understand the services better.
- **Increase mentor feedback:** More project reviews and Q&A sessions would help participants learn from their mistakes.
- **Encourage more networking activities:** Group discussions, experience-sharing sessions, and in-person meetings would help participants connect with each other.

## 4. Acknowledgments and Future Expectations

I would like to thank the organizers, mentors, and everyone who shared their knowledge and supported me throughout FCAJ.

I hope the program continues to grow and provide more opportunities for students to learn, gain practical experience, and connect with others.

The knowledge and experiences I gained from FCAJ will be valuable as I continue my studies and development in Information Technology.
