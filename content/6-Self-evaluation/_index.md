## 2. Knowledge and Experience Gained

Through the program, I learned about AWS services and how they are used in real projects. Working on Tindy helped me better understand system architecture, databases, and cloud application deployment.

I also improved my self-learning, research, documentation, and presentation skills. Participating in community activities, especially Community Day, gave me opportunities to learn from others, meet people with similar interests, and gain new perspectives on the IT industry.

## 3. Areas for Improvement

- Continue studying AWS and gaining more hands-on experience with cloud services.
- Become more independent and proactive when solving problems.
- Improve communication, teamwork, and coordination skills.
- Manage my time more effectively when working on multiple tasks.

## 4. Personal Reflection

FCAJ was a meaningful learning experience for me. The program not only helped me gain AWS knowledge but also gave me opportunities to work on a project, connect with others, and learn from real-world experiences.

Although I still have many areas to improve, I believe I have made progress in my knowledge, skills, and working habits. These experiences have provided a useful foundation for my continued learning and development in Information Technology.
